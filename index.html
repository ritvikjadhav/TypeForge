FAQ

Common questions

What does WPM mean?

WPM means words per minute. It is the standard measurement used to describe typing speed.

How is accuracy calculated?

Accuracy measures how many of your typed characters match the expected text.

Can I save my typing results?

Yes. Once accounts are implemented, your results, personal records and progress will be saved to your profile.

Do I need an account to take a test?

No. You can start a typing test without creating an account.
